01 / SCIENTIFIC INTERESTS

Questions that drive me.

How germline genetics, cellular context, and computational tooling meet to explain human disease.

01✳

Population-scale single-cell genomics

Understanding how transcriptional states vary across hundreds of individuals and across disease-relevant tissues — moving from reference atlases to statistically powered population studies.

02⌁

Linking GWAS loci to effector genes

Most complex-disease GWAS signals sit in non-coding regions. Using cell-type-resolved eQTL mapping, interaction QTLs, and colocalisation helps pinpoint the right gene in the right cell type and context.

03↗

Genetics-informed drug discovery

Drug targets backed by human genetic evidence are substantially more likely to succeed in the clinic. At Open Targets, I’m excited about bringing single-cell functional genomics cleanly into target prioritisation.

04⌘

Open, reproducible science & mentoring

Clear workflows, documented pipelines, and welcoming scientific communities make better research possible. I enjoy mentoring students, running workshops, and sharing genomics with broader audiences.
