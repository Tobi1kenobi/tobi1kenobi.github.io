04 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Curiosity, code
& collaboration.

I’m a single-cell data scientist at EMBL-EBI’s Open Targets, working to bring expression and QTL data into therapeutic target prioritisation.

Previously, I worked in the Anderson Lab at the Wellcome Sanger Institute, where I co-led analyses for IBDverse. I hold a PhD in Mathematical Genomics and Medicine from the University of Cambridge and an MSc in Bioinformatics from the University of Copenhagen.

Beyond research, I enjoy mentoring, building open-source tools and sharing genomics with wider audiences.
